Our Love

Right now, we are in love… 🤍 And this is how we feel our love should be. 🫶
Our happiness should be simple,but our love should be deep.Whether we smile together or fight sometimes, not even for a single day should our hearts drift apart. 🥺
No matter what happens in the future,we should always stand as a team. We can walk holding hands,but we should never let go of each other. 🤍
We don’t need to show our love to the world,feeling it is more than enough. The world doesn’t have to understand us,if we understand each other, that itself is everything. 💫🌙✨
